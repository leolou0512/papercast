Here's how MatterGen steers toward the material you actually want. At every denoising step, it runs the same network twice: once told the target, say a bulk modulus of two hundred gigapascals, and once told nothing. The guided score equals the plain score, plus gamma times the difference, conditioned minus plain. With gamma at one, that's just the conditional model. Turn gamma up, and it leans harder into the target, trading diversity for control.
